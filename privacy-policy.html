ClueSearch may display ads using Google AdMob. Ad providers may process identifiers and usage data according to their own policies.

On iOS, the app may request tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You can change your choice anytime in iOS Settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared with service providers only to operate the app (for example, ad delivery).

5. Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device unless you remove the app or clear device data.

6. Children's Privacy

If you believe a child submitted personal data, contact us and we will review and remove information when applicable.
