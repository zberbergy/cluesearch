Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClueSearch may process, how it may be used, and what choices you have.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Gameplay progress and local preferences stored on your device.
  • Advertising related signals from third-party services (such as Google AdMob), where applicable.
  • Information you choose to provide when contacting support by email.

2. Advertising

ClueSearch may display ads through third-party providers such as Google AdMob. These providers may process device identifiers and limited usage information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

3. Tracking Permission (ATT)

On iOS, ClueSearch may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. If you allow tracking, your device data may be used by ad partners to provide more relevant and personalized ads. If you deny permission, the app may show non-personalized ads instead. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers only as needed to operate and deliver the app (for example, ad delivery).

5. Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device unless you remove the app or clear your device/app data. Third-party service providers may retain data according to their own retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

ClueSearch is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will review the request and take appropriate action.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: magnetoryus@gmail.com
